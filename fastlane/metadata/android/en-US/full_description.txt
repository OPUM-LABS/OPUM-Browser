OPUM Browser is a fast, private web browser that you can shape to the way you hold your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Open source.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting: what you do stays on your device
• Web Filter blocks ads, trackers, pop-ups, cookie banners and annoying overlays, with the filter lists you choose (uBlock Origin, EasyList, AdGuard and more)
• A clear page tells you when the Web Filter blocks a site, with one tap to go back or allow it
• Incognito mode with its own tabs, and an optional lock
• App lock with fingerprint, face or PIN
• Encrypted backups protected by a password only you know
• No Google Play Services needed

<b>Yours to shape</b>
• Put the bars at the top or the bottom, choose the buttons and group them, edit the menu
• Gestures on the screen edges for back, forward and switching tabs
• Light, dark and dark web pages; follow your wallpaper colours or pick your own colour, text size and font
• Every setting can be found with the settings search

<b>Tabs that stay tidy</b>
• Tab overview as carousel, grid, list or stack, with tab groups
• Workspaces: separate sets of tabs and logins, for example for work and home
• Pages zoom smoothly into their card and back
• Throw a tab away with a flick, undo when you close one by mistake
• Close duplicates and old tabs in one go, or let unused tabs archive themselves

<b>Read, watch, listen</b>
• Reader mode for clean articles
• Translate pages and text
• Read pages aloud
• Video helpers: full-screen controls, speed, picture-in-picture, background playback

<b>Handy tools</b>
• Screenshots in one tap, with share, edit and long screenshots of whole pages
• QR code scanner in the address bar
• Desktop sites with one switch, for one tab or all of them
• Download manager with pause, resume and notifications
• Save pages, print to PDF, viewers for images, PDF and text
• Start page with your shortcuts and optional news

<b>For everyone</b>
• Works on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape
• Large text, right-to-left languages and screen readers are supported
• Available in 26 languages
